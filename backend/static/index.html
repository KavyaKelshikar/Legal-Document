LawLens AI System Guide & Viva Walkthrough

verified 1. What is LawLens AI?

LawLens AI: Intelligent Legal Document Classification and Analysis System is designed to analyze, classify, evaluate risk, and answer questions from legal contracts using deterministic algorithms without relying on expensive, hallucinatory Large Language Models (LLMs).

Text Ingress & Segmentation: Document parser extracts text streams, and the NLP engine segments raw text into distinct contractual clauses using sentence boundaries and numbered headings.
AI Clause Classification: AI Classification Engine classifier predicts clause types: Payment, Confidentiality, Termination, Liability, General with ~75% test accuracy.
Mamdani Fuzzy Logic Risk: Computes Importance, Ambiguity, and Risk Indicators into triangular/trapezoidal membership functions to defuzzify into exact 0-100 risk scores.
Semantic Retrieval & Dialogflow: Transforms user questions into semantic vector space, retrieves relevant clauses, and extracts the exact answer sentence.

rule 2. Risk Level Interpretation

  • LOW RISK (0 – 34): Standard balanced commercial provisions with clear obligations and mutual remedies.
  • MEDIUM RISK (35 – 64): Ambiguous terms, vague notice periods, or mild unilateral obligations requiring clarification.
  • HIGH RISK (65 – 100): Asymmetric indemnity, uncapped consequential liability, or harsh unilateral termination covenants.
